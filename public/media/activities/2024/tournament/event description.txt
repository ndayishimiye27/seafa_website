[P] 🔴⚽️ Umugwi w'umupira w'amaguru #SEAFA uhurikiyemwo abize kw'ishure Saint Esprit (Saint Esprit Alumni Football Academy) waratunganije ihiganwa ifise intumbero yo kugwiza ubucuti ku migwi isanzwe ikunda kurangurira imirimo mu bigo vyo kuri Saint Esprit
------------------------
🔥 Iryo higanwa ryatanguye igenekerezo rya 4 Gitugutu rirangira ku wa 3 Munyonyo 2024, rikaba ryahuje imigwi 6 irimwo ibiri ya SEAFA, umugwi w'iryo shure ugizwe n’abanyeshure bahiga, abavyeyi n’abigisha baho, umugwi wa Académie Zoé hamwe n'iyindi migwi ibiri ihurikiyemwo abaririmvyi n’abakorezi bo mu misa baho kw'isengero 
👌🏾 Kari akaryo ko gushira hamwe iyo migwi itandukanye y’abakinyi n’abaririmvyi begukira Ikigo c’Abajezwiti (Jésuites) mu #Burundi, ndetse binonore imitsi biciye mu rukino rw’umupira w’amaguru
📌 Ibirori vyo kwugurura iryo higanwa vyuguruwe n’uwutwara iryo shure Patiri Désiré Yamuremye. Kubwiwe, "n'aho ibibuga vyo gukinirako atako vyifashe, abanyeshure barahimirizwa kwinonora imitsi kuko ku wa gatanu inyuma y’umutaga arivyo vyahagenewe"
👥 Tony Ezako, umukuru w'umugwi SEAFA nawe ati: "Icivugo c'umugwi wacu ni “Ntidukina umupira w’amaguru dukina ivyiyumviro”, kandi hambavu yo gukina hari iyindi ntumbero yo gushira hamwe abafasha ishure n'abigeze kuryigako, tutibagiye abipfuza kuzorifasha muri kazoza kugira bashigikire mu migambi itandukanye y'iryo shure"
🤩 Arongera ati: "Muri kazoza twipfuza gukora amahiganwa azoza araba buri mwaka, akazohuriramwo abantu canke amashirahamwe akorera mu bisata vyegereye canke bijanye n'indero, hamwe n'abasanzwe bafasha canke bipfuza gufasha mur'ico gisata"
🔷 Arnaud Ndagara, yashitse mur'uwo mugwi mu 2016 aciga mu mashure yisumbuye, amenyesha ko intumbero y'amahiganwa azokurikira ari ugukinira ku kibuga ciza kiriko umusezero umeze neza, ivyo bikazoca mu bikorwa vyo gutangura gukora ico kibuga co kw'ishure rya Lycée Saint Esprit"
✅ Abandanya ati: "SEAFA yaramfashije kwitahura mu bijanye no kwitwara neza, na cane cane mu gufasha umugwi ndimwo mu bisata ivyo arivyo vyose kugira nshike kure"
🫱🏽‍🫲🏾 Jimmy Jambo nawe ari mu batanguye kuwukinamwo kuva muri 2016 aramwunganira ati: "Narirya umugwi wacu ushira imbere ugusabikanya, mu mahiganwa azokurikira tuzokwegeranya abantu bafise ubumenyi butandukanye kugire dushire hamwe inguvu, ivyo navyo bikazogira ingaruka nziza bivanye n'imigambi twiha mu buzima na cane cane mu mugwi wacu, aho hari abantu b'imico myiza baza babonye uko natwe twitwara"
📌 Ihiganwa ryarangijwe n'urukino rwa nyuma (finale) rugizwe na SEAFA-A hamwe na SEAFA-B, aho SEAFA-A igizwe n'abawigeze bo mur'uwo mugwi ari yo yatahukanye intsinzi. Iyo ntsinzi ni nk'ubutumwa bwo ku migwi mishasha buyimenyesha ko ari ugushira imbere ubumenyi
🩺 SEAFA ibicishije mu gisata kijanye no kuvura SEAFA médecine, kirimwo abakorera mur'ico gisata, caratanze agashimwe kw'ishure Saint Esprit kagizwe n'ibikoresho vyo kuvura
🔰 SEAFA (Saint Esprit Alumni Football Academy) ryatangujwe muri 2013 n'abanyeshure baryizeko, kugira bateze imbere ubuvuzi biciye mu kwinonora imitsi no kwigishanya umuco wo gufatana mu nda, hamwe vyose bikajana no gutera imbere mu bumenyi arivyo vyamye biranga aba Jésuites barongoye iryo shure
🫂Ubwa mbere SEAFA yajamwo abize kuri Saint Esprit gusa, ariko uwo mutima wo gufashanya watumye bugururira imiryango n'abandi bose, yaba abagore canke abagabo. Kubera abantu batandukanye bavuye no mu bisata bitandukanye vy'ubuzima, uwo mugwi waratunganije inyigisho ku banyeshure baho ku vyigwa bitandukanye, aho twovuga nk'ibijanye no kwitaho amagara meza biciye mu kwinonora imitsi, ariko kandi n'ibijanye no guhitamwo neza ivyigwa bibereye ku banyeshure bazoba bahejeje kur'iryo shure
