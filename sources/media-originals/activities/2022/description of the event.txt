Urwaruka ruhurikiye muri Saint Esprit Alumni Football Academy,  (#SEAFA) ruti : " Turakina agapira ariko amagara y'abantu na canecane urwaruka ntituyasiga inyuma "
📌 Ivyo vyashikirijwe na Muganga Arnaud Favina wo muri #Seafa mu nyigisho yahaye abanyeshure bo kuri Lycée ya Mutima Mweranda #LycéeduSaintEsprit ku civugo "Abayabaga n'ibiyayuramutwe (inzoga n'ibindi)" kuri uno wa gatanu 03 Ruheshi 2022 mu nyubakwa zo kuri iryo shure nyene 
🎙️Yarongeye ati: " Imiyabaga irabangamirwa n'ibiyayuramutwe, n'akaborerwe. Bamwe babijamwo bibaza ko ari imwe mu nzira iheza ibibazo vyabo, ariko bikarangira bibaviriyemwo ingorane ku magara yabo. Ni vyiza rero ko abantu bakurira iyo miyabaga, bicarana bakayaga, bakigishanya mukuru agutahura muto, muto akigira kuri mukuru "
👏🏻 Muganga Badogomba Arnaud yatanguje #Seafa nawe agira ati: " Uwize neza n'uwugisha ivyo azi akabisangiza abandi. Tubitanguriye kuri rino shure kubera ariho twashinze imizi ariko n'ayandi mashure tuzoyagendera "
👏🏾 Bamwe mu rwaruka bari muri izo nyigisho bagira bati :" Kubona abadukurira baza kutwigisha, bakaduha impanuro ni akarorero keza. Natwe twipfuza kubikuriza duhejeje amashure yisumbuye dusangize abandi ubumenyi tuzoba dufise"
